About the project

On a Sunday morning in October, members of the club were joined by Interactors, Rotaractors and residents of Juhu to clear plastic and other waste from two kilometres of the beach.

Volunteers worked in teams along the shore with gloves and sacks, and sorted what they collected into recyclable and non-recyclable waste. The recyclables went to a recycling partner; the rest was handed over to the municipal ward for disposal.

Before the drive began, a short talk showed how plastic dropped in the city’s drains is carried out to sea, and what every household can do to keep it out of the water.

The club plans to repeat the clean-up every month until the monsoon, and invites anyone who lives or works near Juhu to join the next drive.
