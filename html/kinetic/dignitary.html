Olayinka Hakeem Babalola

  • President
  • Rotary International 2026-27

Biography

Olayinka Hakeem Babalola, a member of the Rotary Club of Trans Amadi, Nigeria, was selected by the Board of Directors to become Rotary International’s president for 2026-27. His term began on 1 July 2026.

Babalola began his Rotary journey in 1984 as a Rotaractor and joined the Rotary Club of Trans Amadi ten years later. He has served as district governor (2011-12), RI vice president (2019-20) and a member of the RI Board (2018-20), and on committees including the End Polio Now Countdown to History Campaign Committee and the Nigeria National PolioPlus Committee.

An engineer, he worked for 25 years in the oil and gas industry, holding senior positions at Shell, and founded two companies. He supports The Rotary Foundation with a named endowment and as an Arch Klumph Society member.
