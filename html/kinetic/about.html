Rotary District 3141 brings together 125 Rotary clubs and more than 7,000 members across Mumbai, Thane and Palghar. Our clubs are neighbours, professionals and community leaders who meet every week to plan service that lasts.

Each club chooses its own projects, from health camps and school sanitation to tree plantation and disaster relief. The district connects those clubs, so that good ideas travel, large projects find partners and every Rotarian has a place to learn and lead.

Rotary International is a global network of 1.4 million members in more than 46,000 clubs. Together we work on the causes that matter most to our communities, guided by The Four-Way Test in everything we think, say and do.

The Four-Way Test

Of the things we think, say or do

  1. 1Is it the TRUTH?
  2. 2Is it FAIR to all concerned?
  3. 3Will it build GOODWILL and BETTER FRIENDSHIPS?
  4. 4Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?

Our areas of focus

  • Promoting peace

    Training peacebuilders and supporting dialogue in communities affected by conflict.

  • Fighting disease

    Health camps, screenings and treatment that reach people who would otherwise go without.

  • Clean water and sanitation

    Water filters, toilets and hygiene education in schools and villages.

  • Saving mothers and children

    Neonatal care, nutrition and support for mothers before and after birth.

  • Supporting education

    Classrooms, learning material, scholarships and teacher training.

  • Growing local economies

    Skills, livelihoods and small business support for families.

  • Protecting the environment

    Plantation drives, beach clean-ups and waste reduction.
