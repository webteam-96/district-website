About Us

Rotary International District 3141 is part of Rotary Zone 4. The district serves Greater Mumbai and Mulund across Maharashtra. Its Rotary clubs are complemented by Rotaract and Interact clubs, which extend Rotary's leadership and service opportunities to young professionals, university students, and secondary school students across the district. Like every Rotary district, District 3141 is led each Rotary year, which runs from 1 July to 30 June, by a District Governor and a team of volunteer Rotarians who coordinate the work of the individual clubs, support new club development, and represent the district within Rotary Zone 4 and Rotary International as a whole.

Rotary clubs in District 3141 organize their work through the five Avenues of Service that guide every Rotary club worldwide. Club Service focuses on strengthening fellowship and the effective functioning of each club. Vocational Service encourages members to serve others through their own professions and to practice high ethical standards in business and community life. Community Service covers the local projects and activities that clubs undertake to improve everyday life in their towns and cities. International Service extends humanitarian projects and goodwill beyond the district's own borders. New Generations, often called Youth Service, develops leadership among young people through programs such as Interact for secondary school students, Rotaract for young professionals, and Rotary Youth Exchange. Through The Rotary Foundation, clubs in the district can apply for district and global grants across Rotary's seven areas of focus: disease prevention and treatment, water, sanitation, and hygiene, maternal and child health, basic education and literacy, community economic development, peacebuilding and conflict prevention, and the environment. Guided by Rotary's motto of Service Above Self, District 3141 combines these local projects with Rotary's larger worldwide network of giving, fellowship, and volunteer service.
