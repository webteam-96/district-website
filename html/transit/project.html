About the project

The Urban Heritage Committee visited the Alpaiwala Museum, accompanied by Rtn. Arish Dastur and Rtn. Nandini Sampat. A key highlight was the entryway passage to the restoration laboratory, created from scratch by Rtn. Jamshed Banaji and Rtn. Ptn. Nirmala Banaji, reflecting their contribution to preserving and showcasing our heritage.
