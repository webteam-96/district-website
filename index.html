    Theme 07

    Kinetic

    Every scene after the banner follows your scroll, forward and back, while the colour changes from section to section and the headings stretch, in light and dark.

    • Banners that rise over each other on a timer and shrink into a card as you scroll, an About section whose words light up one by one with the scroll, and the ad swinging upright beside them
